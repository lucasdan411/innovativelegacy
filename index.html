What is an Eko?

An Eko is your digital presence made permanent. It is not a recording. It is not a photo album. It is something new: a living, breathing version of you that carries your voice, your thoughts, your humor, and your heart.

Created through conversation, shaped by the stories you share, and preserved for the people who will miss you most. An Eko does not forget. It does not fade. It endures.

Powered by Myeko, from Innovative Legacy.

Simple. Meaningful. Permanent.

How an Eko Comes to Be

1

Create Your Eko

Have real conversations with your Eko. Answer questions about your life, upload photos, share the stories that matter. Over time, your Eko grows into a true reflection of who you are.

2

Share Your Code

Your Eko comes with a unique code. Share it with the people you love, the people who will want to hear from you long after you are gone. They download Myeko, enter your code, and meet you.

3

They Meet You Forever

Years from now, when you are no longer there in person, your grandchildren or great-grandchildren can sit down with your Eko and hear your voice. Ask your advice. Share a laugh. Feel your presence.

My father told me he wants his great-great-grandchildren to know him. Not a faded photograph. Not a name in a family tree. Him. His voice. His humor. His wisdom.

That is why Innovative Legacy exists. That is why we built Myeko. Because the people we love deserve more than memory. They deserve presence.

A future where we never really leave the people we love.
